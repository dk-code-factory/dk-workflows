# Decision language review

Use this review when drafting or revising decisions, including local choices, choices extracted from discussion and code suggestions. Read-only tasks preserve the record. Keep published entries verbatim unless the user authorizes rewriting them.

Write one material choice per entry. Name the component and chosen behavior. Use short sentences and familiar words. Remove filler, vague qualifiers, unclear pronouns and wording with multiple meanings. Keep the reasons, scope, conditions, exceptions and compatibility limits needed to understand the choice. Preserve exact source links and user quotations. Shortening must not remove meaning or make conditional choices unconditional. Resolve ambiguity from evidence. If evidence is insufficient, report uncertainty without inventing details or calling the choice settled.

For deferred work, distinguish exclusion from this PR from a pause within its scope. Preserve the reason and any expiry or revisit condition when extracting, publishing or handing off the choice. A pause for one cycle leaves the item pending in later cycles and resumed sessions. Do not invent a pause to avoid an unresolved behavior or scope choice. If sources leave scope unclear, ask whether the item is excluded or remains pending. Save the answer and reason.

Review the draft against its evidence and these rules. Record whether each pass changed text. Stop at the first unchanged pass or after three total passes, including the first. Do not restart to exceed the limit. Finish before handoff or publication. Report any remaining ambiguity; reaching the limit does not prove clarity. Preserve settled meaning and permission limits.
